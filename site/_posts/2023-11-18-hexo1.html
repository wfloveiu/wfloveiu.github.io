Hexo+Butterfly中鼠标放到头像上头像会转动，如何取消转动

这个在_config中改不了，经过查阅资料，发现在 hexo-theme-butterfly/source/css/_layout/aside.styl中进行更改,大约在324行

img
  width: 100%
  height: 100%
  transition: filter 375ms ease-in .2s, transform .3s
  object-fit: cover

  &:hover
    transform: rotate(360deg)

将后两行删掉

img
  width: 100%
  height: 100%
  transition: filter 375ms ease-in .2s, transform .3s
  object-fit: cover

这样头像就不会转动了
